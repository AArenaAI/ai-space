# 基于zkp技术实现安全、低gas、通用的spv

## Progress

Currently, we support transaction validity proofs for Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, and zkSync. In addition to this, we also provide services for historical block validity proofs and account state proofs.

##### 优势：

1. 低gas：由于我们使用plonk对halo2进行递归证明，所以**目前在链上证明的gas消耗平均在35w**，而将所有数据上传到链上在合约中计算的gas消耗最少也是300w gas（为什么知道后面一个的gas消耗？因为我们之前就走过这条路）
2. 安全：不管是DA层有效性证明还是L1层有效性证明，最终都会回归到区块证明，这势必会与链上数据挂钩，所以只有正确的数据才能使区块证明通过，且最终的计算的有效性都是在zk证明中得到验证的
3. 通用：我们支持ethereum、arbitrum、optimism和zksync era的证明，未来我们会支持更多的rollup网络，同时未来允许第三方服务使用我们的服务

##### 改进点：

1. 时效性：我们不是实时对链上的数据进行zk证明，而是当有人发起需求时，我们才对需求进行zk证明，所以时效性对我们来说非常重要。目前我们的时效性受两方面影响：一是各个rollup在L1层更新对应状态合约的频率；二是我们链下进行zk计算的复杂度，时间成本随着复杂度的上升而增加。现在，链下zk计算生成证明的时间普遍在于2h~4h(测试环境:128G/64C)，不过我们仍然在优化电路的生成证明时间，目前有两个方向，一是对电路本身进行优化，二是试图进行硬件加速，如gpu，尽快实现电路的生成证明时间在10分钟以内
2. gas消耗：目前我们使用plonk对halo2进行递归证明，不过为了减少链上证明的gas消耗，我们正在试图使用fflonk对halo2实现递归证明

## 介绍

The zk-SPV design we have developed aligns with the description provided in the earlier Yellow Paper：Zero Knowledge Simple Payment Verification. Prove the existence and rationality of Orbiter cross-chain Tx through zero-knowledge proof technology.Existence means that both source transaction and target transaction can be proved on L1 that they actually happened on the corresponding L2, and rationality refers to the ability to prove the user’s intention in SrcTx and ensure that the results of the payment made by the maker in DstTx comply with specific rules.

There are two types of rollups: optimistic rollups and zk-rollups. Their implementations are quite different, Which also leads to the difference of the respective SPV implementations. The specifics of each Rollup’s SPV implementation will not be discussed in this context.Instead, we will utilize a standardized set of proof primitives to universally represent our SPV components. Reducing the gas consumption of transaction validity proofs by adopting ZK-SNARK cryptographic technology.

Moreover, this document will solely focus on the technical implementation of SPV and will not involve any preconditions or postconditions.

## Background

**SPV**: Simplified Payment Verification, firstly proposed in the BitCoin’s whitepaper. It allows a transaction recipient to prove that the sender has control of the source funds of the payment they are offering without downloading the full Blockchain, by utilising the properties of Merkle proofs

**Storage Trie**: Storage trie is where *all* contract data lives. There is a separate storage trie for each account. To retrieve values at specific storage positions at a given address the storage address, integer position of the stored data in the storage, and the block ID are required. 

**Transactions Trie**: There is a separate transactions trie for every block, again storing `(key, value)`pairs.A path here is: `rlp(transactionIndex)` 

**Receipts Trie**: Every block has its own Receipts trie. A `path` here is: `rlp(transactionIndex)`. `transactionIndex` is its index within the block it's mined. The receipts trie is never updated. Similar to the Transactions trie, there are current and legacy receipts. To query a specific receipt in the Receipts trie, the index of the transaction in its block, the receipt payload and the transaction type are required. The Returned receipt can be of type `Receipt` which is defined as the concentenation of `TransactionType` and `ReceiptPayload` or it can be of type `LegacyReceipt` which is defined as `rlp([status, cumulativeGasUsed, logsBloom, logs])`.

## Model

### Logical model

![截屏2023-07-14 14.57.35](/Users/changsheng/Desktop/截屏2023-07-14 14.57.35.png)

The entire proof process is divided into seven steps (referring to the longest steps to be taken during the proof).

1. Proof of L2 receipt validity
2. Proof of L2 transaction validity
3. Proof of L2 block header validity
4. Proof of L1 transaction validity
5. Proof of L1 block contract state validity
6. Proof of L1 block header validity
6. On-chain contract verification

Among them, steps 1, 2, and 3 are referred to as L2 layer validity proof, steps 4, 5, and 6 are referred to as L1 layer validity proof, and step 7 is on-chain contract verification.

Therefore, the **Complete proof of validity** consists of **L2 layer validity proof** + **L1 layer validity proof**.

### Physical model

1. API:A set of off-chain RPC clients designed for SPV (Simplified Payment Verification) services. Externally, their function is to receive transaction and other information and generate zk-SNARK (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Argument of Knowledge) proofs of validity. Internally, they are responsible for constructing input data for circuits and persisting zk-SNARK proofs.
2. Circuit:ZK circuits and libraries based on the Halo 2 proof system.
3. Contract verification:On-chain verification contract for validating zk-SNARK proofs of validity.

## Analysis

### Scenario assumption

To provide a more accessible explanation focusing solely on how to achieve a complete proof of validity for an L2 transaction (i.e., the existence of the transaction, as described in the Yellow Paper), without delving into our specific business context:

Suppose we have an L2 transaction hash, denoted as $H_{s}$. We need to prove on the L1 layer that $H_{s}$ indeed occurred on L2 and was successfully executed. This proof is crucial for us to proceed with further business processing. However, we only possess the L2 transaction hash. How can we accomplish this?

### Details

#### Proof of L2 layer effectiveness

##### L2 Receipt Validity Proof

由于同一个block的Receipts Trie的key和Transactions trie的key是一一对应的，所以相同key的value是同一笔交易的对应数据。即在$H_{s}$的Block $B_{s}$中，$H_{s}$的交易是$T_{s}$,$H_{s}$的收据是$R_{s}$。

要证明$H_{s}$，就要证明$H_{s}$是否执行成功。已知一笔交易的收据中的Status字段表示着交易的执行状态，即可以通过Status字段来判断$H_{s}$是否执行成功；同时要使用MPT实现证明$B_{s}$的Receipts Trie的特定path上存在$R_{s}$,这里的特定path是指$H_{s}$在$B_{s}$中的顺序$I_{s}$，还有，通过API生成的merkle proof $M_{s}$ ,$B_{s}$的Receipts Trie的根哈希为$MRH_{s}$。这些数据作为电路输入，不需要设置输出以及公开输入。


$$
{\widehat{P}}(R_{s},I_{s},M_{s},MRH_{s})
$$

##### L2 Transaction Validity Proof

当然，我们也需要使用MPT证明$B_{s}$的Transactions Trie的特定path上存在$T_{s}$,且$B_{s}$的Transactions  Trie的根哈希为$MTH_{s}$

这些数据作为电路输入，其中将$T_{s}$作为公开输入以便后续链上验证使用
$$
{\widehat{P}}(T_{s},I_{s},MTH_{s})\longrightarrow T_{s}
$$


##### Proof of L2 block header validity

然后，这一步需要证明从$B_{s}$到特定可信区块是连续的。块是严格有序的 (每个创建的新块都包含对其父块的引用)，所以每个块都包含着其父块的哈希值（ParentHash字段），区块的哈希值的生成是对其自身的rlp编码数据进行keccak。

基于L2机制,可以肯定L1层一定有对应的状态合约保存特定状态，所以这里可信区块的来源均来自各个rollup官方提供的链上合约，如arbitrum的outbox合约，optimism的L2OutputOracle 合约，zksync的Storage合约等。

因此我们可以从$B_{s}$开始计算上一个的区块哈希是否等于下一个区块的父哈希值，不断计算直到可信区块$BT_{l2}$($BT_{l2} \ge B_{s}$)。

![截屏2023-07-13 23.11.06](/Users/changsheng/Desktop/截屏2023-07-13 23.11.06.png)

这些数据作为电路输入，其中将$BT_{l2}$作为公开输入以便后续链上验证使用
$$
{\widehat{P}}(B_{s} ,BT_{l2} )\longrightarrow BT_{l2}
$$

#### Proof of L1 layer effectiveness

##### Proof of L1 transaction validity

即使$H_{s}$在完成L2层有效性证明之后，和L1层有什么必然联系吗？

每一笔L2交易都会被各个rollup维护的定序器打包提交到L1层上，区别仅在于L1层记录的是L2交易的哪一部分数据，如arbitrum、optimism记录的是压缩后的L2交易的原始数据（arbitrum采用brotil，optimism采用的是zlib），zksync era记录的每一个batch下所有transactions产生的状态差异

所以$H_{s}$被定序器打包提交到L1层的交易哈希是$H_{sub}$,其交易是$T_{sub}$

接下来需要证明L2交易确实存在于L1的提交哈希中，即证明$H_{s}$的交易信息(可能是交易哈希，也有可能是交易原始数据)存在于$H_{sub}$的calldata中，这部分我们已经实现了arbitrum、optimism的解码器(zksync era我们采取的是nonce验证)

当然，我们也需要使用MPT证明$H_{sub}$所在的block $B_{sub}$的Transactions Trie的特定path上存在$T_{sub}$,path是指$H_{sub}$在$B_{sub}$中的顺序$I_{sub}$,且$B_{sub}$的Transactions Trie的根哈希为$MTH_{sub}$、merkle proof $M_{sub}$ ,

这些数据作为电路输入，不需要设置输出以及公开输入。
$$
{\widehat{P}}(H_{s} ,H_{sub},T_{sub},M_{sub},MTH_{sub},I_{sub} )
$$

##### Proof of L1 block header validity

对L1交易进行交易有效性证明后，同理，我们也需要证明从$B_{sub}$到特定可信区块$BT_{l1}$($BT_{l1} \ge B_{sub}$)是连续的。

为了使链下计算的时间成本降低以及链上可操作性，我们有一个L1StorageBlockHash合约，预期情况下，它会以每小时一次的频率记录当时最新的L1层的区块哈希，并以hash->uint256 的映射形式存在。该合约无任何权限，所有人均可进行更新区块哈希，目前由我们官方进行维护，之后会对更新状态的人进行激励，以鼓励他人参与更新状态。由于这些区块哈希是直接在合约中通过blockhash()读取的，所以可以认为这些区块哈希是有效的。（其实也有考虑过使用默克尔树，但不是太符合我们的业务）

这些数据作为电路输入，其中将$BT_{l1}$作为公开输入以便后续链上验证使用
$$
{\widehat{P}}(B_{sub} ,BT_{l1})\longrightarrow BT_{l1}
$$

##### Proof of L1 block contract state validity

将这部分放到最后进行描述是因为这部分在证明过程中是可选的

智能合约将数据存储在区块链上的长期内存中,内存布局以uint256->uint256的映射形式存在，这种布局被称之为存储插槽。所以我们可以通过存储插槽去获取其中存储的值，只要我们能准确知道特定合约的内存布局方式，以及能够准确计算出存储插槽的key，那么就很轻松实现合约状态的证明。(即证明特定区块下的特定合约的特定状态变量的值)

当然，最后也需要进行Proof of L1 block header validity，从需要证明的区块哈希到可信区块（来源L1StorageBlockHash合约）

由于在假设场景中未使用到合约状态证明，所以这里简单概括一下合约状态证明的电路

将需要证明的合约地址记为$A$,需要证明的存储插槽的 key 记为$S_{k}$(可手动计算，也可使用我们提供的计算slot key的快捷工具，不过该工具在逐渐完善中),需要证明的存储插槽的 value记为$S_{v}$,需要证明的区块哈希或者区块号记为$B_{a}$,可信区块记为$BT_{a}$($BT_{a} \ge B_{a}$)

这些数据作为电路输入，其中将$S_{k}$、$S_{v}$、$B_{a}$、$BT_{a}$作为公开输入以便后续链上验证使用
$$
{\widehat{P}} (A,S_{k},S_{v},B_{a},BT_{a})\longrightarrow S_{k},S_{v},B_{a},BT_{a}
$$

##### On-chain contract verification

在上述步骤完成后，将生成ZK 有效性证明，将其作为$P_{zk}$

同时将上述步骤中的公开输入或输出（如果你定义了）一并传入到验证合约中进行链上验证，验证通过后，即在L1层证明$H_{s}$确实发生在L2层且成功执行。最终结果（公开输入或输出）可以在你的智能合约中以无需信任的方式使用。
$$
{\widehat{V}}(P_{zk} , params)\longrightarrow bool
$$
